Design Choices

Colour: Deep blue represents trust and professionalism, while orange highlights important actions and buttons.

Typography: A system font stack provides fast loading, and 1.6 line-height makes paragraphs easier to read.

Layout: Content is limited to 800px so the page remains comfortable to read. White cards on a light-grey background create a clean appearance.

Maintainability: CSS variables store the colours and border radius, making future design changes easier.
